Who We Are

We Spark Journeys That Last a Lifetime

The Travel Spark was founded by Naresh Shah with a single mission: to make extraordinary travel accessible to every Indian family. What started as a boutique travel consultancy in 2012 has grown into one of the region's most trusted travel companies.

We specialize in fully customised international holidays, premium group tours with confirmed departures, and seamless corporate travel management. Every itinerary we create is built around your unique preferences, budget, and travel style.

From the first enquiry to your safe return home, our team of passionate travel experts is with you every step of the way. That's the Travel Spark promise.
