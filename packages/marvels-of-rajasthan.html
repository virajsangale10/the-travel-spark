✨ Tour Overview

Immerse yourself into the royal heritage of India with our completely customized 9-day Marvels of Rajasthan tour. This journey takes you across the most iconic historical destinations of Rajasthan including the Pink City of Jaipur, the camel country Bikaner, the golden sands of Jaisalmer, and the Blue City of Jodhpur. Enjoy scenic historical tours, majestic forts, royal palace experiences, and magical desert safaris.

📅 Detailed Itinerary

1

Arrival in Jaipur

Jaipur
Arrive in Jaipur and transfer to Hotel. This afternoon proceed on a city tour of Jaipur - visit the city palace, an overwhelming complex of exquisite palaces, gardens and courtyards, the Palace Museum which houses collections of rare manuscripts, armoury, costumes, carpets and miniature paintings. Walk to the adjacent Jantar Mantar, astronomical observatory 17th century. Lastly, drive past the Hawa Mahal or the Palace of Winds. Overnight stay at hotel.
2

Amber Fort & Local Sights

Jaipur
This morning drive to the outskirts of Jaipur city to visit the Amber Fort, an ancient capital of Jaipur where you will see magnificent palaces with walls covered in mirrored mosaics. Enjoy an elephant or jeep ride at the Amber Fort (On Own), Birla Temple. Rest of the day is at leisure for shopping and own activities. Overnight stay at hotel.
3

Drive to Bikaner

Bikaner
Early morning, drive to the imposing walled city of Bikaner, on arrival check-in at Hotel. Rest of the day at Leisure. Overnight stay at hotel.
4

Bikaner Sightseeing

Bikaner
Today post breakfast, proceed for Bikaner Local Sightseeing. Visit Junagarh Fort, Camel Breeding Farm, Deshnok Karni Mata Temple etc. Rest of the day is free for leisure activities. Overnight stay at hotel.
5

Drive to Jaisalmer & Camel Ride

Jaisalmer
Today post breakfast, check out from your hotel & proceed to the desert town of Jaisalmer. Evening enjoy Camel Ride courtesy your Hotel. Overnight stay at Jaisalmer Camp.
6

Jaisalmer Local Sightseeing

Jaisalmer
Today after breakfast, Check out from Camp and proceed for Jaisalmer town. Later proceed for Jaisalmer Local Sightseeing covering Golden Fort, Gadhisar Lake, Patwaon-Ki-Haweli, Nathmalji-Ki-Haweli followed by shopping in Local Market. Overnight stay at Jaisalmer Hotel.
7

Drive to Jodhpur

Jodhpur
This morning drive to the Blue City of Jodhpur enroute visit the Desert Cultural Centre. On arrival check-in at Hotel. Rest of the day is at leisure. Overnight stay at Jodhpur Hotel.
8

Jodhpur Local Sightseeing

Jodhpur
This morning you will visit Umaid Bhawan Palace & Museum, Majestic Mehrangarh Fort, sprawled across a 125 meter high hill and is the most impressive and formidable fort in the fort-studded Rajasthan, continue to Jaswant Thada a cluster of Royal cenotaphs built in 1899. Overnight at Jodhpur Hotel.
9

Departure from Jodhpur

Jodhpur
Departure transfer to Jodhpur Airport/ Railway Station.
