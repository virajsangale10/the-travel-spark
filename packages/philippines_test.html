? Tour Overview

Discover the perfect blend of rich history and tropical paradise on this 7-day Vibrant Philippines tour. Start your journey in the bustling metropolis of Manila, exploring its Spanish-colonial architecture and vibrant streets. Then, fly to the world-renowned island of Boracay, famous for its powdery white sands, crystal-clear waters, and electrifying nightlife. It’s an unforgettable island getaway packed with culture and endless sunshine!

?? Detailed Itinerary

1

Mabuhay! Arrival in Manila

Manila
Arrive at Ninoy Aquino International Airport in Manila. Our representative will meet you and transfer you to your hotel. Welcome to the bustling capital of the Philippines! Check-in to your hotel, relax, and enjoy the rest of your day at leisure. You may explore nearby shopping malls or try out local Filipino delicacies.
2

Old Manila City Tour

Manila
After breakfast, embark on a half-day guided tour of Old Manila. Visit the historic walled city of Intramuros, the defensive core of Spanish Manila. Explore Fort Santiago, a citadel that served as a prison for the national hero Jose Rizal, and marvel at the magnificent San Agustin Church and Manila Cathedral. Return to the hotel in the afternoon or choose an optional shopping tour to SM Mall of Asia.
3

Manila to Boracay Island

Boracay
Enjoy breakfast before checking out of your hotel. You will be transferred to the airport for a short domestic flight to Caticlan or Kalibo. Upon arrival, take a short land transfer followed by a scenic boat ride across to the stunning Boracay Island. Check in to your beach resort, walk along the famous White Beach, and soak in the legendary sunset.
4

Boracay Island Hopping Tour

Boracay
After breakfast, get ready for a fun-filled Boracay Island Hopping Tour. Sail on a traditional Paraw (outrigger boat) and explore the pristine beaches around the island. Visit Puka Shell Beach, Crocodile Island for incredible snorkeling, and enjoy a delicious BBQ picnic lunch. Return to the resort by mid-afternoon. Spend your evening experiencing the island's famous fire dancers and vibrant nightlife.
5

Day at Leisure & Water Sports

Boracay
Today is yours to explore Boracay at your own pace. You can lay back on the fine white sand, take a dip in the ocean, or book optional water sports such as parasailing, helmet diving, banana boat rides, or scuba diving. Treat yourself to a world-class beach massage to truly loosen up and relax.
6

Tropical Leisure & Sunset Cruise

Boracay
Enjoy another leisurely breakfast. Spend your final full day capturing beautiful photos and buying souvenirs at the D'Mall. In the late afternoon, optionally join a gorgeous Sunset Yacht Cruise, sipping a cocktail while watching one of the most famous and breathtaking sunsets in the world dip below the horizon line.
7

Departure

Boracay
Enjoy a hearty tropical breakfast. Check-out of the resort and take the boat and land transfers back to the airport for your flight back to Manila, and subsequently connect to your international flight back home, carrying wonderful memories of the Vibrant Philippines!
