Mesmerizing Maldives

✨ Tour Overview

Escape to a tropical paradise at the Holiday Inn Resort Kandooma, nestled in the pristine South Male Atoll. Surrounded by world-class diving sites and superb surfing breaks, this serene island escape features breathtaking ocean views, crystal-clear warm waters, and luxurious over-water or beach-facing villas. The perfect getaway to unwind, rejuvenate, and forge unforgettable memories under the Maldivian sun.

🏝️
South Male Atoll
4 Nights

📅 Detailed Itinerary

1

Arrival in Maldives

Maldives
Arrive at Velana International Airport (Male). Upon clearing immigration, our representative will warmly welcome you and escort you to the speedboat terminal. Enjoy a scenic 45-minute speedboat ride across the azure waters to the beautiful Holiday Inn Resort Kandooma. Check in, settle into your luxurious villa, and spend the rest of the day at leisure exploring the island or relaxing on the white sandy beaches.
2

Day at Leisure — Island Life

Maldives
After a sumptuous buffet breakfast, the day is entirely yours. You can bask in the sunshine by the spectacular infinity pool, take a refreshing dip in the turquoise lagoon, or explore the vibrant house reef through complimentary snorkeling. For lunch and dinner, enjoy a wide variety of local and international cuisines served at the resort's diverse dining venues.
3

Relaxation or Adventure

Maldives
Start your day with breakfast overlooking the stunning seascape. You may choose to indulge in a rejuvenating treatment at the Kandooma Spa (at an additional cost) or engage in thrilling water sports like windsurfing, kayaking, or diving. The evening offers a perfect opportunity to chill out at the rooftop bar while witnessing a breathtaking Maldivian sunset.
4

Leisure & Optional Excursions

Maldives
Enjoy another day in paradise. Relax under a palm tree with your favorite book or opt for exciting excursions organized by the resort, such as a romantic sunset cruise or a dolphin-watching safari (additional charges apply). In the evening, savor a grand farewell dinner with your loved ones, recounting the beautiful memories created over the past few days.
5

Departure

Maldives
After a delicious final breakfast at the resort, it's time to bid goodbye to this piece of paradise. Check out of your villa and hop onto the speedboat for your return transfer to Velana International Airport, carrying home spectacular memories of your Maldivian holiday.
