Spectacular Dubai

✨ Tour Overview

Witness the futuristic skyline, thrilling desert adventures, and grand shopping experiences that Dubai has to offer. This action-packed 6-day Spectacular Dubai tour takes you from traditional dhow cruises to the world's tallest building, the Burj Khalifa, along with an unforgettable evening roaring through the golden sand dunes on a Desert Safari. A perfect blend of culture, modernity, and adrenaline!

🏙️
Dubai
5 Nights

📅 Detailed Itinerary

1

Arrival in Dubai & Marina Dhow Cruise

Dubai
Arrive at Dubai International Airport. After completing immigration and customs, our local representative will transfer you to your hotel. Check-in and relax for a while. In the evening, get ready to experience the mesmerizing Marina Dhow Cruise. Sail along the dazzling Dubai Marina, enjoying the spectacularly illuminated skyline, accompanied by a lavish international buffet dinner and soft music. Return to the hotel for an overnight stay.
2

Half-Day Dubai City Tour & Burj Khalifa

Dubai
After breakfast, embark on a half-day guided tour of Dubai. Drive past iconic landmarks such as the Jumeirah Mosque, Burj Al Arab, and The Palm Jumeirah. Witness the contrast between the old and the new as you drive along the Dubai Creek. In the afternoon, visit the legendary Dubai Mall, followed by a trip to the 124th floor of the Burj Khalifa (Non-Prime Hours). Enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the entire city. Return to your hotel in the evening.
3

Thrilling Desert Safari with BBQ Dinner

Dubai
Enjoy a relaxed morning and breakfast at the hotel. In the mid-afternoon, our 4x4 Land Cruiser will pick you up for an exhilarating Desert Safari. Experience dramatic dune bashing across the golden sands. After sunset, head to a traditional Bedouin camp to experience camel riding, henna painting, and shisha. The evening comes alive with mesmerizing belly dance, fire shows, and a Tanoura show, complemented by a delicious BBQ buffet dinner.
4

Abu Dhabi City Tour & Grand Mosque

Abu Dhabi
After breakfast, set off for a full-day tour to Abu Dhabi, the capital city of the UAE. Drive past the futuristic skyline and the stunning Corniche. The highlight of the day is a visit to the magnificent Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, a breathtaking architectural masterpiece and one of the largest mosques in the world (strict dress code applies). Make photo stops at Emirates Palace and Ferrari World before returning to Dubai.
5

Leisure & Optional Shopping

Dubai
Breakfast at the hotel. This entire day is yours to enjoy at leisure. Dubai is a shopper's paradise; you may choose to visit the Gold Souk for traditional jewelry or the modern Mall of the Emirates. Alternatively, you can book an optional excursion to visit the beautiful Dubai Miracle Garden & Global Village (seasonal) or experience the snowy slopes of Ski Dubai.
6

Departure

Dubai
Enjoy your final breakfast at the hotel. Check-out and complete any last-minute shopping. Our representative will transfer you to Dubai International Airport for your flight back home, carrying a bag full of spectacular memories.
