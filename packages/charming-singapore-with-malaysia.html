Charming Singapore with Malaysia

🕒 7 Nights / 8 Days
🌍 2 Countries / 2 Cities
👥 Ideal for Family & Kids

✨ Tour Overview

Experience the vibrant tapestry of Southeast Asia with our meticulously crafted "Charming Singapore with Malaysia" tour. This 8-day journey takes you through the futuristic marvels of Singapore and the cultural heartland of Kuala Lumpur.

Singapore
4 Nights
🗼

📅 Detailed Itinerary

1

Arrival & Night Safari / Bird Paradise

Singapore
Welcome to Singapore! On arrival, complete formalities & transfer to the hotel. In the evening, visit the Singapore Night Safari, the world's first nocturnal zoo, offering an immersive wildlife experience. Alternatively, explore Bird Paradise with over 3,500 spectacular feathery residents or visit River Wonders.
2

Singapore City Tour & Leisure

Singapore
After breakfast, enjoy a half-day city tour covering Merlion Park, Fountain of Wealth, Chinatown, Little India, City Hall, and Parliament House. Shop for souvenirs and try local street food. The afternoon is free for you to explore Orchard Road or Bugis Street for budget-friendly shopping.
3

Sentosa Island & Gardens by the Bay

Singapore
Ride the Singapore Cable Car to Sentosa Island. Enjoy the S.E.A. Aquarium, exhilarating Luge & Skyride, and the spectacular Wings of Time show. In the afternoon, visit Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome & Cloud Forest) and the Marina Bay Sands Sky Park Observation Deck for panoramic city vistas.
4

Additional Excursions

Singapore
Enjoy another day of thrilling excursions. Visit additional domes in Gardens by the Bay or explore more of Marina Bay Sands. Relax and soak up the world-class cityscape of Singapore at your own pace.
5

Transfer to Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur
After breakfast, transfer via coach to Malaysia. Upon arrival in Kuala Lumpur, you'll be picked up and transferred to your hotel. Spend the rest of the day at leisure, exploring the beautiful metropolitan city on your own.
6

Kuala Lumpur City Tour

Kuala Lumpur
Begin your Malaysian adventure with a city tour. Visit the King's Palace, National Monument, National Mosque, and Independence Square. Take photos at the iconic Petronas Twin Towers and visit the KL Tower Observatory (421m high) for breathtaking panoramic views of the city skyline.
7

Genting Highlands & Batu Caves

Kuala Lumpur
After a sumptuous breakfast, head to the Genting Highlands hilltop resort via an exciting cable car ride. Enjoy the indoor theme park and Casino de Genting. En-route, visit the majestic Batu Caves, one of the largest Hindu temples outside India dedicated to Lord Murugan.
8

Departure

Kuala Lumpur
Enjoy your last morning in Malaysia. After breakfast, check out of your hotel and transfer to the airport with bags full of souvenirs and happy memories.
