✨ Tour Overview

Discover the soul of the Caucasus on this 5-day journey through Georgia. From the cobblestone lanes of ancient Tbilisi and the spiritual heartland of Mtskheta, to the vine-covered valleys of Kakheti and the dramatic mountain scenery of Kazbegi — Georgia is a destination that surprises and enchants at every turn.

📅 Detailed Itinerary

1

Arrival in Tbilisi

Tbilisi
Arrive in Tbilisi and be welcomed by your local representative, who will transfer you to your hotel. Georgia's ancient and vibrant capital spreads across both banks of the Mtkvari River, surrounded on three sides by mountains — the name "Tbilisi" derives from the Old Georgian word meaning "warm," with human settlement in the area dating back to the 4th millennium BC. Check in to your hotel and spend the rest of the day at leisure.
2

Mtskheta & Old Tbilisi Walking Tour

Tbilisi
Breakfast at the hotel. At around 10:00 am, drive to Mtskheta — the ancient capital of Georgia and one of its most important spiritual and cultural centres. Visit Jvari Monastery and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, then head to Trinity Cathedral, one of the largest in the world. Afterwards, explore Old Tbilisi on foot: the walking tour begins at Europe Square and takes in Metekhi Church, a cable car ride up to Narikala Fortress, the iconic "Mother of Kartli" statue, a stroll down through Abano District, and a walk to the scenic Legvtakhevi Waterfall. Continue along Rustaveli Avenue — the city's main boulevard — and finish with shopping at the Flea Market and Gallery Mall.
3

Kakheti Region & Sighnaghi

Tbilisi
Breakfast at the hotel. At around 10:00 am, drive to the Kakheti Region — Georgia's celebrated "Cradle of Wine," famous worldwide for its winemaking heritage and unique grape varieties. En route, stop in the village of Badiauri to taste freshly baked local bread and cheese. Continue to Sighnaghi, the charming "City of Love," perched on a hilltop with sweeping views of the valleys and the Greater Caucasus Mountains. Visit the Bodbe Nunnery before returning towards Tbilisi. Upon request, guests may stop at the KTW Winery for a wine tasting. On the way back, visit the historic Ujarma Castle.
4

Gudauri & Kazbegi (Weather Permitting)

Tbilisi
Breakfast at the hotel. Early start at 09:00 am for the Mtskheta-Tianeti Region tour. Visit the Ananuri Fortress Complex and Jinvali Dam along the scenic Georgian Military Highway. Continue to Gudauri and stop at the viewpoint monument for a stunning photo opportunity. If weather conditions permit, drive on to Kazbegi, switch to a 4x4 vehicle, and ascend to the legendary Gergeti Trinity Church set against a dramatic mountain backdrop. If weather does not permit travel to Kazbegi, enjoy free time in Gudauri for snow activities before returning to Tbilisi.
5

Departure

Tbilisi
Breakfast at the hotel. After checking out, your representative will transfer you to the airport for your onward flight — heading home with wonderful memories of Georgia's mountains, monasteries, and warm hospitality.
