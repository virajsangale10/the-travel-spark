✨ Tour Overview

A grand sweep across the finest of Europe — this 15-day journey takes you through six iconic countries, each with its own character and charm. From London's regal landmarks and Paris's timeless romance, to the alpine majesty of Switzerland, the medieval grandeur of Cologne, the cosmopolitan pulse of Brussels, and the canal-laced beauty of Amsterdam — this is Europe at its most magnificent, all in one seamless, fully-inclusive tour.

📅 Detailed Itinerary

1

Arrival in London

London
Arrive in London and meet our representative for a transfer to your hotel. Check in and relax after your journey. In the evening, enjoy a delightful Indian dinner. Overnight in London.
2

London City Tour, London Eye & Thames Cruise

London
After breakfast, proceed for a guided city tour of London covering all the major landmarks. Enjoy lunch at an Indian restaurant, then head to the iconic London Eye for sweeping views of the city, followed by a scenic Thames River Cruise. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in London.
3

Madame Tussauds & Oxford Street Shopping

London
After breakfast, visit the world-famous Madame Tussauds wax museum. After lunch, enjoy leisure time shopping along Oxford Street — one of London's most iconic shopping destinations. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in London.
4

Eurostar to Paris — Eiffel Tower

Paris
After breakfast, transfer to the station to board the Eurostar train to Paris. On arrival, enjoy lunch at an Indian restaurant before heading to the iconic Eiffel Tower (2nd Level) for panoramic views over the City of Light. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Paris.
5

Paris City Tour, Seine River Cruise & Louvre

Paris
After breakfast, enjoy a guided city tour of Paris covering all the major highlights. Lunch at an Indian restaurant. Later, take a romantic Seine River Cruise drifting past the city's grand facades, and make a photo stop at the magnificent Louvre Museum. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Paris.
6

Paris Leisure Day / Optional Disneyland® Paris

Paris
After breakfast, enjoy the day at leisure, or opt for a full-day optional excursion to the magical Disneyland® Paris (EUR 95 per person). Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Paris.
7

Drive to Zurich

Zurich
After breakfast, depart by road for Zurich with a packed lunch en route. On arrival, check in at the hotel and settle into one of Europe's most beautiful cities. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Zurich.
8

Mount Titlis Excursion & Lucerne Orientation

Zurich
After breakfast, proceed for a thrilling excursion to Mount Titlis, including the exhilarating Ice Flyer chairlift experience over the glaciers. Later, enjoy an orientation tour of the charming lakeside city of Lucerne. Return to Zurich for dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Zurich.
9

Zurich Leisure Day / Optional Jungfraujoch

Zurich
After breakfast, the day is free for leisure — explore Zurich's old town or lakefront at your own pace. Alternatively, opt for an optional excursion to the legendary Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe (EUR 220 per person), the highest railway station in Europe at 3,454m. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Zurich.
10

Rhine Falls, Black Forest & Drive to Cologne

Cologne
After breakfast, visit the majestic Rhine Falls — the largest waterfalls in Europe — with a scenic boat ride. Continue to the legendary Black Forest region and visit the Drubba Cuckoo Clock Factory. Enjoy lunch at an Indian restaurant, complete with the region's famous Black Forest Cake. Drive on to Cologne and check in. Packed dinner provided. Overnight in Cologne.
11

Drive to Brussels — City Orientation

Brussels
After breakfast, drive to Brussels — the heart of Europe. Enjoy lunch at an Indian restaurant, then embark on an orientation tour of the city covering its key attractions including the Grand Place and the Atomium. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Brussels.
12

Amsterdam — City Tour & Canal Cruise

Amsterdam
After breakfast, proceed to Amsterdam. Lunch at an Indian restaurant, followed by a guided city tour of Amsterdam and a scenic canal cruise through the city's famous waterways. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Amsterdam.
13

Zaanse Schans Windmills & Dam Square

Amsterdam
After breakfast, visit Zaanse Schans — a living open-air museum famous for its traditional windmills, wooden houses, and quintessential Dutch countryside charm. Lunch at an Indian restaurant, followed by free time to explore Dam Square and the surrounding streets. Overnight in Amsterdam.
14

Madurodam & The Hague Beach

Amsterdam
After breakfast, visit Madurodam — the world-famous miniature city park showcasing the Netherlands in miniature detail. After lunch at an Indian restaurant, proceed to The Hague for a leisurely afternoon at the beach. Dinner at an Indian restaurant. Overnight in Amsterdam.
15

Departure from Amsterdam

Amsterdam
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and transfer to Amsterdam Airport for your return flight home — carrying unforgettable memories of six magnificent European countries.
