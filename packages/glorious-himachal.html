✨ Tour Overview

Escape to the breathtaking landscapes of Himachal Pradesh with our 6-day Glorious Himachal tour package. Begin your journey in the beautiful colonial hill station of Shimla followed by a remarkable stay amidst the soaring peaks in Manali. Enjoy scenic drives through pine and deodar forests, spiritual retreats, and picturesque sightseeing in the Himalayas.

📅 Detailed Itinerary

1

Arrival in Shimla

Shimla
Arrive at Chandigarh Airport / Station and depart for Shimla by road. Shimla the state capital of Himachal Pradesh is surrounded by pine deodar, oak, and rhododendron forests. Shimla was also the summer capital of the British Raj. Arrive Shimla and check into your hotel. Overnight at Hotel.
2

Shimla Excursion & Local Sights

Shimla
Morning post breakfast, proceed for an excursion to Kufri & later visit Himachal State Museum, Vice Regal Lodge, Jakoo Hill, Lakkar Bazaar & Walk through the Mall Road. Overnight at Hotel.
3

Transfer to Manali

Manali
After early breakfast get transferred to Manali. Transfer through the beautiful mountains and settle in. Overnight at Hotel.
4

Manali Local Sightseeing

Manali
This morning visit Hadimba Temple, Manu Mandir, Vashisth Kund, Tibetan Monastery & Handicraft Centre. Rest of the day is at leisure. Overnight at Hotel.
5

Solang Valley

Manali
Post Breakfast visit Solang Valley OR Optionally visit Rohtang Pass (On Own). Overnight at Hotel.
6

Departure from Chandigarh

Chandigarh
After early breakfast drive to Chandigarh, on arrival Transfer to Chandigarh Airport / Railway Station for onward journey.
