10 Travel Hacks Every Indian Traveller Should Know

Traveling internationally from India can seem daunting, but a few smart strategies can save you money, time, and stress. Here are 10 travel hacks every Indian traveler should know.

1. Get a Zero Forex Markup Card

Stop paying 3.5% markup fees on international swipes. Cards like Niyo Global or specific credit cards offer zero forex markup, saving you thousands on long trips.

2. Book Flights on Tuesday or Wednesday

Airlines often release sales early in the week. Browsing in incognito mode and booking mid-week can often yield the cheapest fares.

3. Utilize Airport Lounge Access

Many Indian debit and credit cards offer complimentary domestic and international lounge access. Enjoy free food, Wi-Fi, and comfortable seating while you wait for your flight.

4. Pack Universal Adapters and Extension Cords

Different countries have different plug sockets. A universal adapter is a must. Bring a small Indian extension cord so you only need one adapter to charge multiple Indian devices.

5. Carry Ready-to-Eat Meals

If you have strict dietary requirements or just crave a taste of home, carrying a few packets of instant Poha, Upma, or Maggi can be a lifesaver on days when local food doesn't suit you.

6. Download Offline Maps

Before you leave your hotel Wi-Fi, download your destination on Google Maps for offline use. You'll never get lost, even without a local SIM card.

7. Check Visa-Free or E-Visa Countries

Maximize your Indian passport. Countries like Thailand, Malaysia, Maldives, and Mauritius often offer visa-free entry or easy E-visas, saving you tedious paperwork.

8. Buy Travel Insurance

Never skip travel insurance. It costs a fraction of your trip budget but can save you lakhs in case of medical emergencies, lost baggage, or trip cancellations.

9. Carry Extra Passport Copies and Photos

Keep a few photocopies of your passport and visa, plus passport-sized photos, in different bags. If you lose your passport, this makes getting a replacement at the embassy much faster.

10. Use Local Ride-Hailing Apps

Instead of expensive airport taxis, use local apps. Grab in Southeast Asia, Bolt in Europe, or Uber globally. They offer transparent pricing and safety tracking.
