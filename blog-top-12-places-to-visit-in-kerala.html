Top 12 Places to Visit in Kerala

Known as "God's Own Country," Kerala is a tropical paradise in southern India. From tranquil backwaters to misty hill stations, here are the top 12 places to visit in Kerala.

1. Munnar

Famous for its rolling tea estates and misty mountains, Munnar is a haven for nature lovers and romance seekers.

2. Alleppey (Alappuzha)

Experience the iconic Kerala backwaters on a traditional houseboat cruise. The serene canals and palm-fringed shores are unforgettable.

3. Wayanad

A lush, green district offering waterfalls, spice plantations, and wildlife sanctuaries. Don't miss the Edakkal Caves.

4. Kochi (Cochin)

A vibrant coastal city blending history and modernity. Explore Fort Kochi, the Chinese Fishing Nets, and the local spice markets.

5. Thekkady

Home to the Periyar National Park, this is the best place in Kerala to spot elephants in the wild and enjoy jungle treks.

6. Kumarakom

A quieter alternative to Alleppey, Kumarakom is situated on Vembanad Lake and is renowned for its bird sanctuary.

7. Kovalam

A famous beach destination with its iconic lighthouse. Perfect for surfing, sunbathing, and Ayurvedic treatments.

8. Varkala

Known for its stunning cliffside views overlooking the Arabian Sea, Varkala offers a bohemian vibe and pristine beaches.

9. Bekal

Visit the historic Bekal Fort, the largest in Kerala, offering breathtaking views of the coastline.

10. Vagamon

A lesser-known hill station with pine forests, cascading waterfalls, and sprawling meadows—ideal for paragliding.

11. Kozhikode (Calicut)

Steeped in history and famous for its culinary delights, especially the Malabar Biryani.

12. Thrissur

The cultural capital of Kerala, known for its grand festivals, ancient temples, and vibrant art scene.
