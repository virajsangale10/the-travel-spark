Singapore + Malaysia in 7 Days: The Ultimate Itinerary

Combining the futuristic skyline of Singapore with the cultural richness of Malaysia makes for an incredible week-long vacation. Here is the ultimate 7-day itinerary for an unforgettable trip.

Day 1: Arrival in Singapore & Marina Bay

Arrive at Changi Airport (an attraction in itself!). Check into your hotel and spend the evening exploring Marina Bay Sands. Watch the spectacular Gardens by the Bay light show at the Supertree Grove.

Day 2: Universal Studios & Sentosa

Dedicate the day to Sentosa Island. Enjoy the thrilling rides at Universal Studios Singapore, relax at Siloso Beach, and catch the Wings of Time night show.

Day 3: Culture & Shopping

Explore the cultural enclaves of Little India and Chinatown. In the afternoon, shop till you drop on Orchard Road. In the evening, take a bus or brief flight to Kuala Lumpur (KL), Malaysia.

Day 4: Exploring Kuala Lumpur

Start your day at the iconic Petronas Twin Towers. Visit the KL Tower for panoramic views, and in the evening, enjoy the vibrant street food scene at Jalan Alor.

Day 5: Batu Caves & Genting Highlands

Take a morning trip to the Batu Caves to see the massive golden statue of Lord Murugan. Later, head up to the cool climate of Genting Highlands for casino entertainment and indoor theme parks.

Day 6: Malacca Day Trip

Take a day trip to the historic city of Malacca (Melaka). Explore Jonker Street, see the red colonial buildings of Dutch Square, and enjoy a river cruise.

Day 7: Last Minute Shopping & Departure

Spend your final morning shopping for souvenirs at Central Market or Pavilion KL before heading to Kuala Lumpur International Airport for your flight home.
