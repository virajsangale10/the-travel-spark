Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for cruises? +
Yes, depending on the itinerary and your nationality, you may need multiple visas. We assist with all visa processes for every port of call.
Are cruises expensive? +
Cruises offer great value as they include stay, food, and transport between cities. We offer options from entry-level luxury to ultra-premium suites.
What is included in cruise packages? +
Standard packages include accommodation, all meals, port taxes, and onboard entertainment. Alcohol and shore excursions are usually extra unless specified.
Is it suitable for families? +
Absolutely! Modern cruise ships are multi-generational with kids' clubs, teen zones, and activities for all ages.
Best time to go? +
Mediterranean (May-Oct), Singapore (Year-round), Alaska (May-Sept), and Dubai (Nov-March) are the best times respectively.
