First-Timer's Guide to Vietnam for Indian Travellers

Vietnam is fast becoming one of the most popular destinations for Indian travelers, offering stunning landscapes, rich history, and incredible affordability. Here is a first-timer's guide to Vietnam.

1. Visa Process

Indian citizens need a visa for Vietnam. The easiest way is to apply for an E-Visa online, which takes about 3-5 working days. Make sure your passport has at least 6 months of validity.

2. Currency and Budget

The local currency is the Vietnamese Dong (VND). Vietnam is extremely budget-friendly. You can enjoy delicious street food for under and find excellent boutique hotels for - a night.

3. The Food Scene

Vietnamese cuisine is fresh and flavorful. Must-tries include Pho (noodle soup) and Banh Mi (baguette sandwiches). For vegetarians, look for the word "Chay" (vegetarian) – many restaurants offer excellent mock meats and tofu dishes.

4. Getting Around

Download the 'Grab' app—it's the Southeast Asian equivalent of Uber. It's cheap, safe, and saves you from haggling. For intercity travel, domestic flights (VietJet) or overnight sleeper trains are great options.

5. Must-Visit Places

  • Hanoi: The historic capital with its bustling Old Quarter.
  • Halong Bay: Take a cruise among thousands of limestone karsts.
  • Hoi An: A magical, lantern-lit ancient town.
  • Ho Chi Minh City: The modern, energetic southern hub.

6. Weather Considerations

Vietnam is long and narrow, meaning the weather varies drastically from North to South. While it might be snowing in the northern mountains (Sapa), it could be tropical beach weather in the south (Phu Quoc). Pack layers!

7. Language

English is somewhat understood in tourist areas, but downloading Google Translate (with offline Vietnamese downloaded) will be your best friend when ordering food or giving directions.
