Top 10 Things to Do in Paris

Paris, the City of Light, is endlessly rewarding. With limited time, you want to make every moment count. From iconic monuments to hidden gems the guidebooks miss, here are the top 10 things you must do when visiting Paris.

1. Marvel at the Eiffel Tower

No trip to Paris is complete without seeing the Eiffel Tower. For the best experience, visit at sunset or see it sparkle on the hour after dark from the Trocadéro.

2. Explore the Louvre

Home to the Mona Lisa and the Venus de Milo, the Louvre is vast. Don't try to see everything in one day; pick a few wings and enjoy the art at a leisurely pace.

3. Wander Through Montmartre

This historic district was once home to artists like Picasso and Dalí. Walk the cobblestone streets, visit the Sacré-Cœur Basilica, and enjoy the sweeping views of the city.

4. Cruise the Seine River

A boat cruise along the Seine offers a unique perspective of Paris's stunning architecture, especially beautiful as the sun sets and the city lights up.

5. Visit Notre-Dame Cathedral

Though currently undergoing restoration, the exterior of Notre-Dame remains a masterpiece of French Gothic architecture. Walk around the Île de la Cité for the best views.

6. Stroll the Champs-Élysées

Walk from the Place de la Concorde up to the Arc de Triomphe. The avenue is lined with cafes, theaters, and luxury shops.

7. Discover the Musée d'Orsay

Housed in a former railway station, this museum holds the largest collection of Impressionist masterpieces in the world, including works by Monet, Van Gogh, and Renoir.

8. Relax in the Luxembourg Gardens

Grab a baguette, some cheese, and have a quintessential Parisian picnic in these beautiful, formal gardens.

9. Experience Parisian Café Culture

Sit at a sidewalk table at Café de Flore or Les Deux Magots in Saint-Germain-des-Prés, order an espresso, and watch the world go by.

10. Take a Day Trip to Versailles

Just a short train ride away, the opulent Palace of Versailles and its expansive, manicured gardens are an absolute must-see.
